# Unwritten — Two-Minute Demo Script

## Before starting

Keep these open:

- The Unwritten React dashboard
- Superhuman Go with the installed **Unwritten** agent selected
- Grammarly enabled in Chrome

Use fictional application records throughout the demo.

## 0:00–0:20 — The problem

Say:

> College applications contain rules that are written down and expectations that are not. Students without access to institutional guidance can complete the stated requirement and still miss what reviewers assume they know. Unwritten makes those assumptions visible.

Show the dashboard with Redwood College and North Valley University.

## 0:20–0:40 — Select an unfamiliar requirement

Open Redwood College and select:

> Submit two letters of recommendation.

Show its explicit status, deadline context, and unresolved hidden expectations.

## 0:40–1:05 — Use the real Superhuman Go agent

Select **Open in Unwritten Go agent** and copy the grounded prompt.

Paste it into the installed Unwritten agent in Superhuman Go.

The live agent should return:

- What it says
- What the student might not know
- Why it matters
- What to do next
- A follow-up question

Answer the follow-up with:

> Not yet. I do not know who to ask.

This is the central sponsor-powered moment. Clearly state that Superhuman Go is performing the decoding and planning.

## 1:05–1:25 — Review and accept the plan

Return to the dashboard's Decoder demo, choose **Not yet**, and show the personalized proposed tasks.

Select **Accept plan & add tasks**.

Show that the approved tasks appear in **My Tasks** and are connected to the Redwood recommendation requirement.

Complete one task and show progress updating.

## 1:25–1:45 — Open Writing Studio

Open Writing Studio for the recommendation request.

Show that it asks for:

- Recipient
- Relationship
- Opportunity
- Deadline
- A real personal memory

Generate the editable draft and point out that Unwritten leaves a placeholder rather than inventing a personal experience. Demonstrate Grammarly feedback in the browser if available.

## 1:45–2:00 — Optional support and close

Briefly enable Momentum and show a workload-aware supportive message.

Close with:

> Unwritten does not just tell students what to submit. It reveals what institutions assume, explains why it matters, and helps students take the next step independently.

## Claims to avoid

Do not say:

- The React application directly calls a private Superhuman Go API.
- Imported public-page information is automatically verified.
- The app can access login-protected portals from a pasted link.
- Grammarly or Unwritten wrote a student's personal application essay.
- Fictional demo deadlines or policies belong to real institutions.
